Before We Start

What Your Store Needs to Prepare

A few things to have ready — we'll guide you through each one.

Products & Pricing

The heart of your store.

  • Product list with names, prices and descriptions
  • Product photos (clear, well-lit images)
  • Variants — sizes, colours, options (if applicable)

Your Branding

What makes your store look "you".

  • Logo file (PNG with transparent background is best)
  • Brand colours and preferred style

Payment Gateway Account

Needed to accept real payments.

  • A registered account with a South African payment provider
  • We can guide you through setup if you don't have one yet

Shipping & Delivery

How orders reach your customers.

  • Delivery areas and courier or collection preference
  • Shipping rates or free-shipping thresholds
